---
name: Watering checks versus watering cadence
description: FloraWise knowledge must distinguish how often to inspect watering conditions from how often to water.
---

Treat the workbook's watering-check interval as a reminder to inspect conditions, not a fixed watering cadence. If a source-backed watering rule is triggered by soil or plant conditions, do not use an older fixed interval to create overdue watering advice.

**Why:** The FloraWise workbook's «Контроль_поливу_днів» field specifies a check interval, and many watering rules depend on soil moisture rather than a calendar.

**How to apply:** Keep check intervals and watering frequencies in separate fields and labels. Recommendations should present the trigger and suggested action; never convert an inspection interval into a watering schedule.
