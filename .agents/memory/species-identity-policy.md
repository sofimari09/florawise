---
name: Species identity policy
description: Product constraints separating arbitrary personal aliases from botanical identity.
---

Allow arbitrary personal plant aliases, even when they mention a different botanical name. Never restrict an alias with botanical-name heuristics or guess/repair historical species links from names.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires species ID to be the sole botanical authority and forbids inferring identity or repairing historical species IDs from display names.

**How to apply:** Identity selection and validation must use actual existing species records. Keep canonical species identity visible separately from the optional display alias; do not reinterpret an alias in future care, classification, or migration work.
